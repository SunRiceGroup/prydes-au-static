Why EasiFeed One®?

For the Best Results

  • Introduce into a horse’s ration gradually over 2 weeks.

  • Feed additional grains, high energy fibres or oil where extra calories are required. Oats or Pryde’s BioMare Cubes, Energy Pak (extruded corn), Power Pak (extruded barley), EasiFibre or EasiOil are ideal additions. Avoid feeding uncooked corn or barley.

  • Feed in conjunction with ample forage (pasture, hay and/or chaff).

  • Make clean, fresh water and salt available at all times.

  • Adjust the amount fed so it is appropriate for a horse’s body fatness, growth rate and stage of development, along with the quality and quantity of forage available.

  • Divide feeds into two or more smaller meals if feeding more than 0.5 kg per 100 kg of bodyweight in ‘hard feed’ per day.

  • The feeding rates given here are based on the amount of feed needed to meet a horse’s vitamin and mineral requirements. If you feed below these feeding rates, vitamin and mineral deficiencies will appear in the diet and may affect health and limit growth and sound development. To avoid this, simply add one of Pryde’s EasiBalanced Pellets to top up vitamin and mineral intakes.

Keep it Balanced
