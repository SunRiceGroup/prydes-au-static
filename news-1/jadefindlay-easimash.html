Jade Findlay’s Go‑To Feed for Travelling, Conditioning and Happy Horses

December 18, 2025
kylieh@prydes.com.au

Jade Findlay has been competing 4* and 5* Eventing for the last 25 years and also operates a highly successful Thoroughbred business alongside her husband at Hannover Lodge in Wilberforce. 

Findlay manages over 140 horses and was recently appointed Athlete Director by Equestrian Australia. 

It’s fair to say she knows a thing or two about horses and how to get them at their best. 

We caught up with Jade to find out more about her experience with Easimash. 

“We have been using Pryde’s EasiFeeds for a long time, the feeds are top quality and we just get the most amazing results,” she said. 

“I have been competing at 5 Star Eventing for the last 20 to 25 years and a common problem that we have with eventing horses, we travel all around Australia, as you travel the horses go off their feed.

“I was always searching for a product that could help my horses maintain their appetite through these stressful situations.

“I think the first indicator that a horse is under stress is when they go off their feed and their gut is uncomfortable. 

“I started using the EasiMash to help with that. 

“I took my mare to Adelaide a few years ago and normally, I have to take into account that she usually loses weight and loses condition, during the trip and while she is at the event. 

“I used EasiMash the whole way through and it was actually a little embarrassing, by the last trot up, she had actually put on weight, rather than lost weight, so it was a remarkable tool to keep her eating, her energy up, and make sure she had the supplements she needed to keep her in top form.

“It was incredible, I’m careful now not to overfeed her, but it is an incredible product, and helped my competition, but most importantly helped my mare stay happy.”

And EasiMash couldn’t be easier to prepare. 

“The consistency of the mash is really beautiful, it doesn’t turn into mush,” she said.

“My horses absolutely love it. 

“The consistency is amazing and it only takes about 30 seconds to soak in, there is no pre-soaking, or pre-preparing it beforehand. You literally add warm water or cold water and they absolutely love it.”

EasiMash is packed with fibre, lots of quality protein, cool energy sources, and gut-friendly ingredients, that is also balanced with vitamins and minerals and it is delicious. 

A cool energy mash with fibre, stabilised rice bran for calories and added protein to assist with muscle building.Fortified with vitamins and minerals and packed with fibre, protein and energy making it a one feed solution.

It is designed for Pleasure Horses, Performance Horses, Endurance Horses and Aged Horses.
