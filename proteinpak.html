Why ProteinPak®?

100% full fat soybean provides the highest quality protein and richest source of essential amino acids of any plant protein for incredible muscle building capacity.

Omega 3 and 6 fatty acids for brilliant coat shine and skin health.

Rich in essential amino acids including methionine to support healthy and strong hooves.

Rich in branch-chain amino acids (BCAAs) to assist with post-exercise muscle recovery.

ProteinPak does not contain added vitamins and minerals, so it can be added to any ration and fed with any other feed without unbalancing the diet.

For the Best Results

  • ProteinPak® is not a complete feed and must be fed as part of a balanced ration.

  • Introduce into a horse’s ration gradually over 2 weeks.

  • Feed in conjunction with ample forage (pasture, hay and/or chaff).

  • Make clean, fresh water and salt available at all times.

  • Adjust the amount fed so it is appropriate for a horse’s body fatness, stage of training, workload, pregnancy, lactation or development or rate of growth, along with the quality and quantity of forage available.

  • Divide feeds into two or more smaller meals if feeding more than 0.5 kg per 100 kg of bodyweight in ‘hard feed’ per day.

Keep it Balanced

ProteinPak does not contain added vitamins and minerals so it needs to be fed as part of a balanced diet. To meet vitamin and mineral requirements, you should feed ProteinPak together with any of the Cubes, Pellets or Sweetfeeds from the Pryde's EasiFeed range, or use one of the pellets from the Pryde's EasiBalanced range.
